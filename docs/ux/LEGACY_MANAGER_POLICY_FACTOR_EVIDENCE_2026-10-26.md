## Owner's target eras
1970–1991; 1991–1999; 2000; 2001; 2002–2004; 2004–2013. Treat these as **research cohorts** and not definitive policy legal boundaries.

## Official sources
- Clal's annual report (https://www.clalbit.co.il/media/29149/clal-hold-q4-2023-acc-0830.pdf) distinguishes original pension factors for eligible older deposits from separate policy capital/pension riders and contributions since 2000. An alternative pension track may carry a coefficient determined at retirement. Do not infer an insurer-specific coefficient solely from a policy's issue year.
- Harel (https://www.harel-group.co.il/long-term-savings/independent-and-employees/information/allowance-pension-savings) distinguishes guaranteed-return policies through 1990, participating policies from 1991–2003 and investment-track policies since 2004. Product eras are not numeric annuity factor tables.
- Menora (https://www.menoramivt.co.il/general/fluctuation) lists factor documents for Marav, Status and other families; their policy linkage has not yet been validated.

## Direct inspection of user-uploaded Clal workbooks
The 2009 / 3.5% workbook has a plan named "מיטבית" marketed from 1998, but a plan-list note says the annex factor applies to regular contributions beginning 2008-01-01 under continuation rider 1200. Therefore this factor is NOT evidence for the original 1998 contract's full balance. The 2013 workbook lists 1268/1269/1270 program codes and the 2022 workbook lists two new Dynamic Profile families. The 2001 and 2009/3% workbooks require the same policy-level and rider-level eligibility validation.

## Mandatory evidence classes
1. Original guaranteed policy coefficient and precise base route.
2. Later continuation rider applicable to identified deposits.
3. New-deposit or transfer coefficient conditional on contribution date/type.
4. Special pension-track company quotation or planner-authorized manual estimate scoped to one scenario decision.

A policy may have multiple savings tranches with different factor rights. Match insurer, policy name and code, original issue date, edition/rider dates, tranche contribution dates, survivor/guarantee terms, retirement sex/age and documentary source before calculating.
