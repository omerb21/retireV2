## New official primary sources
1. [Menora coefficient archive](https://www.menoramivt.co.il/general/fluctuation) exposes three specifically titled historical guaranteed coefficient XLSX downloads: **Marav**, **Status**, and **Status combined**. This is more directly aligned with original managers-insurance policies than modern 2022/2026 tables. **File contents not yet decoded**. Excel content-type could not be opened through browsing; do not imply numeric validation. Map marketed product/version and actual rider before acceptance.
2. [Harel official 2016 rider / program 514 for insured age 60+](https://www.harel-group.co.il/Policies/נספח%20הבטחת%20מקדם%20גילאי%2060%20פלוס.pdf), PDF p. 12 (physical index 11), Annex B, verified by screenshot. Describes a **60% or 100% lifelong spouse-survivor benefit** (no equation with 240 months of guaranteed payments). The company requires spouse identity/birth date for option B. The table has a 2015 base and a per-year increase, distinct for male/female and age. This is **not evidence for 1970–2001 original policy rights**. It is a separate optional rider for defined policies/age group.
3. [Harel official pension claim options](https://www.harel-group.co.il/long-term-savings/independent-and-employees/claim/withdrawal-severance-pay) says default minimum guaranteed payments differ for policies issued through June 2001 (120) versus from July 2001 (240), according to product conditions.
4. [Harel official policy eras](https://www.harel-group.co.il/long-term-savings/independent-and-employees/information/allowance-pension-savings): guaranteed-return before 1991; participating-profit 1991–2003; investment-track 2004 onward. These are market/product cohorts, not coefficients.
5. [Phoenix 2008 insurer filing](https://digital-content.fnx.co.il/sites/docs/genery/dohotkaspiyim/phnixh408_isa.pdf) describes pre-2004 manager-insurance product family **Adif**, later plans and early July 2001 changes in mortality tables. Corporate filings are contextual, not individual policies.
6. [Clal policy-history disclosure in 2025 annual report](https://www.clalbit.co.il/media/dinpea32/clal-holdings-q4-2025-acc-26032026-0600.pdf) reports an **allegation in litigation** around 2016 increased deposits going to newly opened policies with non-guaranteed factors despite original pre-May-2001 policies; not a finding of insurer misconduct or a universal contract interpretation.
