# RETIRE V2 | Pre-2001 original policy coefficient evidence gate

Date: 2026-10-26. Research only; no implementation or financial result authorization.

## Most useful new discovery

An independently operated historical pension simulator explicitly targets classic Gimla/Adif policies issued between 1983-01-01 and 2001-05-01 and supports seven payout modes: no guarantee; minimum 60 or 120 payments; 25 death benefits; survivor pensions 60%, 80% or 100%. Source: https://theodore.co.il/simulators-pension/ and iframe https://simolator-kitzba-polisot-adif.theodore.co.il/ . **Third-party, not primary insurer evidence.** The site warns the insurer's actual calculation prevails. This is potentially useful as a test reference but cannot justify copying its algorithm or data or treat it as contractual authority.

## Regulatory and issuer corroboration

- Clal official 2020 public report at https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/2020clal/he/CLAL_420_1.pdf says deposit tranches before 2000 in certain policies had conversion by original factor; post-2000 choices between capital and pension contracts/riders; policies marketed before 2001 had several guaranteed pension routes, while from 2001 guarantees applied to a particular route with minimum 240 guaranteed payments, and alternative routes may use factors established at retirement. These are descriptions of Clal products, not universal insurer contracts.
- Migdal's 2005 historical financial statement: https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/2005migdal/he/regulation_MIGDL2005.pdf describes policy-specific actuarial tables and interest applicable to pre-2000 pension elections. Do not infer a universal factor.
- Industry historical context: https://www.policycheck.co.il/המבנה-המיוחד-של-פוליסות-מסוג-עדיף-מוב/ identifies historical Adif product names across companies (Phoenix Maniv, Clal Meitav, Migdal Yoter, Harel Meula, Menora Merav), but this crosswalk is **secondary** and should be checked against source-original policy documents.
- Secondary illustrative numbers https://pensuni.com/?p=1067 are benchmarks only, not guaranteed factors.

## Key product interpretation

The absence of insurer-original tables is a genuine **source-evidence** gap, not a permission to infer a customer's factor from a generic year or copy a third-party simulator. A usable factor requires at least (insurer, policy/program, original contract edition, covered deposit layer, pension mode, sex/age, and relevant beneficiary terms), and reference to a specific source row or manually entered insurer-provided quote. Payout modes such as 60% survivor cannot silently reuse a 120-payment guarantee factor.
