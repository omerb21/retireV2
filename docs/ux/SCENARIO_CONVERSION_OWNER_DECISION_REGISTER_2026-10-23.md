## O-01 owner clarification (2026-10-23)
The owner clarified that education funds and investment provident funds have **no severance components**. The product-type exception was introduced because their balances can be recorded in different contribution columns, including post-2000 columns otherwise disallowed for capital conversion; that classification must not disable an appropriate capital action for these product types. Prior proposed framing as a legal permission to bypass genuine severance restrictions was incorrect. Treat a severance component under either product type as an inconsistent input needing source reconciliation, not a reason to approve conversion. This narrows the O-01 issue; the exact canonical resolver and anomaly handling still need contract review. Do not mark professional tax classification approved.
