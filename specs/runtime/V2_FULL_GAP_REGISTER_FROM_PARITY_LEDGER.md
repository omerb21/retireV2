- `CRITICAL_BLOCKER`: Blocks later milestones or trustworthy behavior, including raw preservation, parsing, ledger authority, conversion factors, tax parameters/engines, scenarios, outputs, audit, E2E, security, or recovery.
- `HIGH`: Materially limits a milestone without immediately blocking the whole system.
- `MEDIUM`: Affects parity, UI completeness, lifecycle, review workflow, or operations and can be scheduled inside its milestone.
- `LOW`: Bounded replacement review or inspection with limited downstream effect.
- `UNKNOWN_RISK`: Only an unknown row whose severity cannot be assigned before inspection.
