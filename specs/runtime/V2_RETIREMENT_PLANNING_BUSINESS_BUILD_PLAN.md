# V2 Retirement Planning Business Build Plan

## 1. Current Direction Reset

Previous RAW-REM and CLOSURE work found useful V1 source knowledge, risk areas, and dependency evidence, but the raw-item proof model became too heavy for practical V2 build planning. Raw V1 source logic coverage remains `FAIL`, and full V1-to-V2 planning completeness under the prior parity definition remains `NOT_PROVEN`.

This document resets the working planning unit from individual technical source items to retirement-planning business modules. V1 remains the primary source of professional, business, and calculation logic. The V2 goal is equivalent retirement-planning logic at module level, with explicit review of material inputs, rules, outputs, warnings, and decisions. The goal is not a technical clone of every V1 behavior.

Execution is `NOT AUTHORIZED` by this file. `02M` remains `FROZEN`. This is a business-module build plan, not a parity proof, implementation package, or acceptance decision.

## 2. Definition of "Logic Must Be Identical"

At the professional and business level, V2 must preserve the material meaning and outcomes of V1 unless a deliberate change is separately documented and approved. That preservation includes:

- required inputs and their source-of-truth rules;
- asset classifications and category boundaries;
- pension versus capital treatment;
- tax, exemption, fixation, and 161D rules;
- scenario calculations and comparison meaning;
- missing-data behavior and blocking conditions;
- warnings, validation outcomes, and professional cautions;
- planner review and recommendation logic;
- client-facing output content and provenance.

Equivalent logic does not require V2 to copy:

- V1 file or package structure;
- V1 UI layout or navigation;
- known V1 bugs or accidental behavior;
- every old test or test fixture;
- internal implementation details that do not change business meaning;
- every historical technical output, log message, or serialization detail.

When exact business behavior is uncertain, the plan records an open question and requires V1 source inspection or a professional decision. It does not fill gaps with assumed formulas or customary tax rules.

## 5. Build Sequence

The practical build sequence is dependency-based, with narrow module planning and acceptance before downstream logic is opened:

1. **Case foundation: M01.** Stabilize client scope, authoritative facts, completeness, ownership, and navigation. Every later record must be client-bound.
2. **Source intake and preservation: M02-M03.** Define approved intake formats, preserve raw evidence, and require review before normalized information becomes authoritative. M03 can be planned while M02 format decisions are resolved, but production intake cannot complete without both.
3. **Classification and ledger: M04-M05.** Classify reviewed records before ledger aggregation. The ledger must retain source links, component boundaries, discrepancy state, and historical snapshots.
4. **Conversion and tax inputs: M06-M07.** Conversion depends on classified ledger data and authoritative coefficient/product rules. Tax inputs depend on client facts, ledger/conversion facts, annual parameters, and source versions. These modules may be planned in parallel but cannot be accepted independently where they share an unresolved classification or parameter rule.
5. **Fixation integration: M08.** Preserve accepted V2 fixation behavior, resolve remaining V1 business rules, and consume authoritative facts from M01 and tax/conversion context from M06-M07. Formal 161D output remains gated by approved form authority.
6. **Scenario execution: M09.** Open only after pension conversion, tax inputs, and fixation interfaces are stable. Scenario runs must lock all inputs and call approved business engines rather than duplicate their formulas.
7. **Comparison: M10.** Compare persisted M09 results only. It must not recalculate tax, pension, conversion, or cashflow values.
8. **Professional review: M11.** Consume M10 evidence and keep human judgment separate from calculations. Recommendation behavior remains blocked until its authority, taxonomy, and approval model are decided.
9. **Client output: M12.** Assemble only selected, reviewed, and approved evidence from M09-M11. Reports depend on scenario comparison and planner review and must never become a second calculation engine.
10. **Cross-cutting trace and release: M13-M14.** M13 contracts begin with M01 and are implemented incrementally across every module. M14 defines module acceptance early, then performs full workflow, professional, security, recovery, rendering, and audit validation after M12-M13 are complete.

The dependency spine is:

`M01 -> M02/M03 -> M04 -> M05 -> M06/M07 -> M08 -> M09 -> M10 -> M11 -> M12 -> M14`

`M13` spans and constrains every step. A downstream module must stop when an upstream source, classification, formula, version, professional decision, or acceptance contract is unresolved.
